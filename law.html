Explore 5-year integrated law programs at National Law Universities (NLUs), corporate deal advisory, constitutional advocacy, international arbitration, and judicial services.

The Modern 5-Year Integrated Law Degree

In India, legal education was revolutionized with the creation of **National Law Universities (NLUs)**, beginning with NLSIU Bengaluru in 1987. Today, students right after Class 12 (from **any stream**—Science, Commerce, or Arts) can enroll in premier 5-year integrated programs:

B.A. LL.B (Hons)
Combines political science, sociology, and constitutional jurisprudence.
B.B.A. LL.B (Hons)
Combines financial accounting, corporate management, and commercial statutes.

Major Specializations in Law

Corporate Law & M&A Advisory
Top Recruiter

Advise conglomerates, private equity funds, and venture capital investors on multi-billion dollar mergers, acquisitions, antitrust compliance (CCI), and initial public offerings. Leading law firms include Cyril Amarchand Mangaldas, Shardul Amarchand Mangaldas, AZB & Partners, and Trilegal.

Courtroom Litigation & Criminal Defense
Courtroom

Represent clients in High Courts and the Supreme Court of India across constitutional disputes, criminal appeals, civil property partition, and public interest litigation (PIL).

Judicial Services (Civil Judge / Magistrate)
Judiciary

Adjudicate disputes from the bench. Law graduates appear for State Judicial Services Examinations to be appointed as Civil Judges (Junior Division) and Judicial Magistrates.

Intellectual Property & Cyber Law
Tech & Law

Protect software patents, trademarks, AI copyright infringement, digital data privacy regulations (DPDP Act), and international trade secrets.

Premier Law Entrance Examinations

CLAT
Common Law Admission Test
Gateway to 24 Consortium National Law Universities including NLSIU Bengaluru, NALSAR Hyderabad, and WBNUJS Kolkata.
AILET
All India Law Entrance Test
Dedicated entrance exam for National Law University, Delhi (NLU Delhi).
