SCIENTIFIC INQUIRY

Science & Research Careers

Discover paths in pure mathematics, quantum astrophysics, molecular biology, environmental climatology, and world-class scientific research laboratories in India.

Beyond Conventional Engineering and Medicine

Many students assume that choosing the Science stream (PCM or PCB) restricts them to either B.Tech or MBBS. In reality, the realm of **pure and applied sciences** offers some of the most intellectual, prestigious, and globally mobile careers in human history.

From uncovering the origins of the universe at ISRO and CERN to synthesizing green hydrogen materials and building quantum cryptography algorithms, research scientists stand at the frontier of civilization.

Core Scientific Domains

Physics & Astrophysics

Cosmology, dark matter, condensed matter physics, quantum optics, particle physics, and satellite systems.

Mathematics & Statistics

Cryptography, financial quantitative analysis, algorithmic game theory, stochastic calculus, and machine learning foundation.

Chemical Sciences

Nanotechnology, novel polymer chemistry, lithium-ion battery electrochemistry, and pharmaceutical drug synthesis.

Biological & Environmental

Genomics, bioinformatics, marine ecology, climate change modeling, and cellular microbiology.

Premier Science Institutes in India

  • IISc Bangalore (Indian Institute of Science): Consistently India's #1 ranked university globally for pure scientific research and doctoral programs.
  • IISERs (7 Centers across India): Indian Institutes of Science Education and Research (Pune, Kolkata, Mohali, Bhopal, Thiruvananthapuram, Tirupati, Berhampur) offering dual BS-MS programs.
  • NISER Bhubaneswar & UM-DAE CEBS Mumbai: Autonomous science research institutions supported by the Department of Atomic Energy.

Entrance Pathways to Pure Science

IAT
IISER Aptitude Test
Admission to BS-MS at IISERs & IISc Bangalore.
NEST
National Entrance Test
Gateway to NISER Bhubaneswar and CEBS.
CUET-UG
Central Universities
Admissions to B.Sc (Hons) in DU, BHU, JNU.
