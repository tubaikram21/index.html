What is NEET?

The National Eligibility cum Entrance Test (Undergraduate), commonly known as NEET-UG, is India's single uniform entrance examination for admission to undergraduate medical programs. Governed by the National Medical Commission (NMC) and administered by the National Testing Agency (NTA), NEET is mandatory for admission to:

  • MBBS: Bachelor of Medicine and Bachelor of Surgery
  • BDS: Bachelor of Dental Surgery
  • AYUSH: BAMS (Ayurveda), BHMS (Homeopathy), BUMS (Unani), BSMS (Siddha)
  • Allied Courses: B.V.Sc & AH (Veterinary), B.Sc Nursing (in select central institutes)

Regardless of whether you seek admission to top central institutions like AIIMS New Delhi and JIPMER Puducherry or state government and private medical colleges, qualifying NEET-UG with a competitive rank is mandatory.

Who Usually Explores This Path?

Students who thrive on the NEET pathway typically demonstrate:

Patient Care Empathy
A genuine commitment to human health, empathy for suffering, and dedication to public welfare.
Memorization & Detail Recall
Ability to master extensive anatomical terminology, botanical classifications, and biochemical cycles.
Long-Term Resilience
Readiness for a rigorous educational journey spanning 5.5 years of MBBS plus 3 years of MD/MS post-graduation.
Calm Under Pressure
Composure during high-stakes practical emergencies, clinical ward rounds, and continuous examinations.

Key Subjects & Marking Scheme

The NEET-UG question paper consists of 200 multiple-choice questions (candidates attempt 180 questions) across three core disciplines:

Subject Questions Marks Focus Areas
Biology (Botany + Zoology) 90 Questions 360 Marks Human Physiology, Genetics, Ecology, Cell Biology, Plant Physiology
Chemistry 45 Questions 180 Marks Organic reaction mechanisms, Physical chemical equilibrium, Inorganic NCERT
Physics 45 Questions 180 Marks Mechanics, Electrodynamics, Modern Physics, Optics, Thermodynamics
Total 180 Questions 720 Marks +4 for correct, -1 for incorrect answer

Proven Preparation Approach

1. Master NCERT as the Primary Bible

Over 90% of Biology and Inorganic Chemistry questions in NEET are taken directly from NCERT lines, summary tables, and diagram captions.

2. Numerical Problem Solving in Physics & Physical Chemistry

Solve previous 15 years' question papers (PYQs) and practice formula speed drills daily to maintain high accuracy within 1 minute per question.

3. Realistic OMR Sheet Mock Testing

Simulate offline pen-and-paper tests strictly between 2:00 PM and 5:20 PM to train your circadian rhythm and prevent bubbling errors on test day.
