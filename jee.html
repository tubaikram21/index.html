Understanding the Two Tiers of JEE

The Joint Entrance Examination is structured into two sequential stages designed to filter and admit candidates into premier Indian engineering universities:

Stage 01

JEE Main

Conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA) in two sessions (typically January and April). It serves two functions:

  • Admission gateway to NITs, IIITs, and Centrally Funded Technical Institutes (CFTIs).
  • Qualifying screening examination for the top 250,000 candidates eligible to write JEE Advanced.
Stage 02

JEE Advanced

Conducted annually by one of the 7 zonal IITs under the Joint Admission Board (JAB).

  • Exclusive admission gateway to the 23 Indian Institutes of Technology (IITs).
  • Highly challenging multi-format exam testing deep analytical derivation, multi-concept synthesis, and partial negative marking.

Subject Breakdown (JEE Main Paper 1)

JEE Main Paper 1 for B.E./B.Tech comprises 90 questions (candidates attempt 75 questions):

Subject MCQs (Sec A) Numerical (Sec B) Total Marks Core Focus
Mathematics 20 5 out of 10 100 Calculus, Coordinate Geometry, Vectors & 3D, Matrices
Physics 20 5 out of 10 100 Mechanics, Electrostatics, Optics, Magnetism, Modern Physics
Chemistry 20 5 out of 10 100 Organic mechanisms, Coordination compounds, Thermodynamics
Total Exam 60 15 Attempted 300 Marks Computer Based Test (3 Hours)

Mechanical Engineering
Automotive EV drivetrains, robotics, aerospace propulsion, thermal systems.
Civil & Infrastructure Engineering
Smart city planning, high-speed rail transit, structural earthquake engineering.

Recommended Preparation Approach

  • 1. Build Intuition Over Formula Cramming: Understand the physical derivations behind formulas. JEE Advanced deliberately tests non-standard question frames that cannot be solved by rote templates.
  • 2. Daily Numerical Solving Quota: Maintain a disciplined habit of solving 40-50 challenging numerical problems daily across Physics, Chemistry, and Math.
  • 3. Rigorous CBT Mock Simulation: Practice navigating computer-based interfaces, marking questions for review, and managing the 180-minute clock with zero panic.
Official Verification Notice: JEE Main and JEE Advanced eligibility conditions, board percentile cutoffs, syllabus topics, and admission guidelines are updated on official notification release. Always verify official updates on jeemain.nta.nic.in and jeeadv.ac.in.
