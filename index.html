Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about navigating streams, exams, and career options.

When should a student start planning their career path?
The ideal time to begin career exploration is during Class 9 and Class 10. This gives students ample time to assess their core academic interests before committing to the Science (PCM/PCB), Commerce, or Arts stream in Class 11.
Can I switch streams or career directions after Class 12?
Yes! Students with a Science background can generally transition into Commerce, Economics, Law, Design, Management (BBA/IPMAT), or Humanities. However, transitioning into medical or core engineering from a Commerce or Arts stream is restricted due to subject prerequisites.
What is the difference between NEET and JEE?
NEET is the single nationwide medical entrance test in India required for MBBS, BDS, and AYUSH courses (demanding Physics, Chemistry, Biology). JEE (Main & Advanced) is the national entrance test for engineering and technical institutions such as IITs, NITs, and IIITs (demanding Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics).
Does CareerAI replace professional career counselors?
CareerAI is designed as an educational exploration tool to empower students with structured insights. We strongly recommend discussing all major academic decisions with parents, qualified career counselors, and school teachers.
How does MHT-CET differ from JEE Main?
MHT-CET is administered specifically by the Maharashtra State CET Cell for admission into engineering and pharmacy colleges within the state of Maharashtra. JEE Main is a central national entrance exam for admission into NITs, IIITs, CFTIs, and top nationwide colleges.
