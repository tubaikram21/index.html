What is MHT-CET?

The Maharashtra Common Entrance Test (MHT-CET) is a state-level computer-based entrance examination conducted annually by the State CET Cell, Government of Maharashtra. It is the primary gateway for admission to:

  • First Year Engineering & Technology (B.E. / B.Tech)
  • Pharmacy Degrees (B.Pharm / Pharm.D)
  • Agricultural & Food Technology Courses (B.Sc Agri / B.Tech Food)

With over 400 government, autonomous, and private technical institutes participating in the Centralized Admission Process (CAP), MHT-CET is an indispensable target for students residing in or seeking technical education across Maharashtra.

The Two Dedicated Pathways

Engineering Track

PCM Group

For students targeting B.E. / B.Tech degrees across Computer Science, AI, Mechanical, Civil, and Electronics.

  • Paper 1: Mathematics (50 questions × 2 marks = 100 marks)
  • Paper 2: Physics & Chemistry (100 questions × 1 mark = 100 marks)
  • Total: 200 Marks (180 Minutes)
Pharmacy Track

PCB Group

For students targeting B.Pharm, Pharm.D, and agricultural/food technology degree programs.

  • Paper 1: Biology (100 questions × 1 mark = 100 marks)
  • Paper 2: Physics & Chemistry (100 questions × 1 mark = 100 marks)
  • Total: 200 Marks (180 Minutes)

Syllabus Weightage & Unique Scoring Dynamic

NO NEGATIVE MARKING

Unlike NEET and JEE, MHT-CET has zero negative marking. This means speed, time management, and the ability to attempt 100% of questions through calculated elimination are critical to achieving a 99+ percentile.

Class 12 Syllabus (~80% Weightage)
The entire current Maharashtra State Board Class 12 textbook curriculum.
Class 11 Syllabus (~20% Weightage)
Selected foundational chapters specifically notified by the CET Cell.
