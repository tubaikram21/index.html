HEALTHCARE DISCIPLINE

Medical & Healthcare Careers

Explore the vast landscape of clinical practice, dental surgery, pharmacotherapy, allied health, and biomedical research dedicated to preserving and restoring human life.

1. Clinical Medicine (MBBS & Beyond)

The flagship route in medical education is the Bachelor of Medicine and Bachelor of Surgery (MBBS), a 5.5-year degree including 4.5 years of academic/clinical curriculum followed by a mandatory 1-year rotating rotatory internship in a recognized hospital.

Doctor of Medicine (MD)
Internal medicine, pediatrics, radiology, pathology, dermatology, psychiatry.
Master of Surgery (MS)
General surgery, orthopedics, ophthalmology, obstetrics & gynecology, ENT.

Diverse Healthcare Specializations

Dentistry (BDS & MDS)

Focuses on oral pathology, teeth alignment (orthodontics), maxillofacial reconstructions, and periodontology. Admission via NEET-UG.

Pharmacy & Pharmacology (B.Pharm / Pharm.D)

Formulation of chemical drugs, clinical trials oversight, bio-equivalence research, and pharmaceutical regulatory affairs. Available via MHT-CET, GPAT, and state tests.

Physiotherapy (BPT)

Neurological rehabilitation, pediatric mobility therapy, orthopedics physical therapy, and elite sports medicine performance training.

Nursing & Critical Care (B.Sc Nursing)

Bedside intensive care management (ICU), surgical theater nursing, patient telemetry, and community public healthcare administration.

Allied Healthcare Sciences

Medical laboratory technology (BMLT), radiology and MRI imaging technology, cardiac perfusion technology, and optometry.

Core Skills for Healthcare Success

Patient Empathy
Gentle bedside manners and active listening skills during moments of acute patient distress.
Physical Stamina
Endurance for 24-hour emergency duty shifts, long surgical procedures, and continuous ward rounds.

NEET Entrance Guidance

All medical and dental admissions in India are routed through the NEET-UG examination.
