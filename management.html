Business & Management Careers

Explore executive leadership, integrated 5-year IPM programs at Indian Institutes of Management (IIMs), startup entrepreneurship, brand marketing, and management consulting.

Leading Teams, Strategy, and Enterprises

Management is the discipline of aligning people, financial capital, and operational processes to achieve extraordinary business outcomes. In India, management education has evolved significantly: students no longer need to wait until after completing an engineering or commerce degree to pursue management.

Through **Integrated Programme in Management (IPM)** pathways pioneered by IIM Indore, students can gain direct entry into prestigious Indian Institutes of Management immediately after Class 12.

Undergraduate Management Pathways

Integrated Programme in Management (IPM) • IIMs
Direct Entry to IIM

A dual-degree 5-year curriculum leading to a BBA + MBA from premier Indian Institutes of Management. Students bypass the post-graduate CAT examination pressure.

Participating IIMs: IIM Indore, IIM Rohtak, IIM Ranchi, IIM Bodh Gaya, IIM Jammu.
Entrance Exam: IPMAT (Indore / Rohtak) and JIPMAT.
BBA / BMS / BFIA Programs
University UG

Three or four-year degrees offered by Shaheed Sukhdev College of Business Studies (SSCBS, DU via CUET), NMIMS Mumbai (NPAT), Symbiosis Pune (SET), and Christ University Bengaluru.

Core Functional Disciplines

Brand Marketing & Growth
Consumer psychology, digital marketing, viral brand campaigns, performance analytics, and product marketing.
Management Consulting
Solve critical strategic dilemmas for Fortune 500 CEOs at McKinsey & Co, Boston Consulting Group (BCG), and Bain & Co.
Talent & Organizational Strategy
Executive compensation, leadership development, corporate culture building, and employee retention programs.
Supply Chain & Logistics
Inventory optimization, global multimodal logistics networks, fulfillment warehouses, and procurement.

Core Executive Skills

  • Strategic Decision Making: Synthesizing incomplete business data to take calculated risks.
  • Negotiation & Communication: Articulating clear visions to stakeholders, investors, and team members.
  • Data-Driven Problem Solving: Interpreting profit-and-loss sheets, CAC/LTV unit metrics, and business dashboards.
